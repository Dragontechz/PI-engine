0. Why this change exists

A 320p frame stretched with DrawTexture / bilinear is pixelated or blurry. Games avoid that by:

    Jittering the internal camera every frame
    Reconstructing each display pixel from that jittered grid (Lanczos/EWA)
    Keeping history at output resolution (UE5 TSR / FSR2)
    Reprojecting that history with MVs that include camera rotation
    Sharpen + grain last

Your job is to make PiTSR do exactly that. Internal 320×180 (or whatever) is a sampler, not the image the player sees.
1. Non-negotiable product rules

    Present only the display-res PiTSR output (UpdateTexture + 1:1 DrawTexturePro). Forbidden: drawing internalRT to the swapchain as the final image.
    History buffers live at displayW × displayH, not internal res.
    Current frame is sampled from internal res with jitter invert + Lanczos-2.
    History is Catmull-Rom gathered from the full plane (camera yaw ≫ tile halo).
    CPU-only kernels. raylib/rlgl/rglfw: window, RT, readback, upload, HUD.
    Do not InitWindow / GLFW a second time.
    Prefer sharp + a little grain over plastic blur. Prefer reject-history over smear.

Target scales: 1.5×, 2×, 3×, and 6× (e.g. 320p→1080p). Must not look like nearest-neighbor blocks at 6× when the camera is still.

Internal iW×iH (inputs only):

    color HDR linear, depth, mv (pixels, internal), optional albedo/normal/roughness/reactive

Persistent display res (ping-pong):

    histY, histCo, histCg (fp16, pre-exposed)

Scratch display res: out*, varPre
Scratch internal: dilated MV

On resize: realloc, resample old hist bilinear once, histLen = 0 on novel pixels. Defer free until workers idle.

If current PiTSR stores history at internal res: migrate to display res. That is the feature.
4. Math to implement (do not stub)
4.1 Jitter

Halton(2,3), 8–16 period, offset [-0.5, 0.5] internal pixels. Inject into projection before BeginMode3D. Save jittered viewProj as next frame’s viewProjPrev.

Reconstruct:
text
Copy

src = (float2(X,Y)+0.5) * (iSize/oSize) - jitterPx

If jitter is 0 or not inverted → pixelation and/or permanent blur. HUD must print jitter.
4.2 Lanczos-2 current (kills fat pixels)

4×4 taps, w = Lanczos(dx)*Lanczos(dy), a=2.
Reject taps |Δz| > τz (τ≈0.02).
Anti-ring: clamp to 3×3 min/max.
Not bilinear. Not nearest. Not DrawTextureEx scale.
4.3 Motion vectors (kills turn-smear)
text
Copy

world = ReconstructFromDepth(uv, depth, invViewProj)
mv_internal_px = (ndc_this.xy - ndc_prev.xy) * 0.5 * iSize

Includes camera. Y sign: yaw right → background MV points left in texture space. HUD F3 color wheel. If mean |mv|≈0 while turning, fix this before any filter work.

Convert for history: mv_out = mv_internal * (oSize/iSize).
Dilate 3×3 longest |mv|, bias min depth.

No depth: homography at focus plane, or whole-frame resetHistory on large camera rotation — never blend unwarped frames.
4.4 History gather (CPU tile bug)

W2/display pass reads Catmull-Rom from full hist plane. Tile halo is only for AABB/à-trous. Off-screen or border taps → disocclude.

Depth reject: |z - z_reproj| > 0.02*z or normal fail → histLen=0, K=K_MAX, do not lerp smear.
4.5 AABB + BATF at display res

YCoCg or OKLab. Expand AABB with sqrt(var) and |mv|. Clamp chroma harder than luma.
text
Copy

K ∈ [0.12, 0.85] toward CURRENT
histLen max 16
if |mv_out|>8  → raise K
if |mv_out|>12 → cap histLen 4
if |mv_out|>25 → cap histLen 2
if histLen<4   → K ≥ 0.5

SpectralBlend: coarse luma may use history; finest band stays close to current (lerp(cur, accum, 0.35) on high freq). Do not TAA reconstructed edges to death.

Demodulate if albedo exists. Firefly: μ+κσ luma.
4.6 Spatial

At ≥3× scale: 0–2 edge-aware iters max. VAR_FLOOR so you never drive noise to 0. Skip spatial on high |mv| tiles. BudgetGate drops spatial first, never Lanczos/history/CAS/grain.
4.7 CAS + StableGrain last

CAS luma only, sharpness 0.6–0.9.
Grain after CAS, luma only, from varPre before spatial, plus GRAIN_MIN so amp never 0. Blue-noise LUT 128², GRAIN_TEMPORAL 0.0–0.2. No rand(). Strength ~0.03.

HUD/UI drawn after PiTSR at native res.

6. raylib / rlgl wiring

    Internal RenderTexture at iW×iH (float/16F if possible; document fallback).
    PiTSR_ApplyCameraJitter on Camera3D / projection each frame.
    Velocity + depth export shader or CPU reconstruct; shaders export only.
    Persistent Image + Texture2D at oW×oH. UpdateTexture main thread.
    Resize: rebuild RTs + PiTSR_Resize. DPI-aware DrawTexturePro.
    Readback ms vs PiTSR ms both in HUD. Keep internal res modest if readback dominates.

7. Scheduling

Same CPU pool. Display-res tiles 32×32. Parallelism across tiles.
History read-all / write-interior. No GL on workers.
Double-buffer FramePacket if overlapping. Deterministic tile order.
